U-100 scale: 100 units = 1 mL. These markings express volume, not peptide mass or biological activity. Other scales differ.
Formulas and worked example

Target mg = target mcg / 1,000
Concentration mg/mL = total mg / volume mL
Target volume mL = target mg / concentration mg/mL
U-100 markings = target volume mL × 100

Example: 10 mg in 2 mL gives 5 mg/mL. A target of 250 mcg is 0.25 mg, corresponding to 0.05 mL and 5 U-100 markings. Check: 0.05 mL × 5 mg/mL = 0.25 mg.
